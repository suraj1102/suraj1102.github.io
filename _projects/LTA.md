## Aerostat Sizing

The task of this assignment was to perform the sizing, and design of the aerostat used in *Sharma, Vishaal & Dusane, Chetan & Verma, Raman. (2019). Design, Fabrication and Testing of an Aerostat System for Last Mile Communication. 10.2514/6.2019-2979.*

Given the operating condition (temp., humidity, wind speed), envelop material properties, payload, aerostate geometry, I determined the necessary volume, forces acting on the system, and other sizing considerations. The effect of using hydrogen or helium as the lifting gas was also analysed.

The full analysis is shown [here](/assets/files/ITDA_Sizing_Asg.pdf).

## ERDD — Emergency Rapid Deflation Device

Most emergency devices on LTA systems like airships and aerostats rely on rupturing the envelop to secure the payload. The task of this assignment was to design a system that would keep the envelop intact while also recovering the payload.
