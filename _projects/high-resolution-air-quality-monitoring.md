### Overview
Air pollution in India is a major challenge, especially in cities where local spikes often go undetected due to sparse monitoring. Existing government stations are too few to capture these variations, and AQI metrics often overlook harmful gases.

Our project tackles this by building a network of affordable, high-resolution IoT air quality monitors across the Tricity region. This will provide detailed, real-time pollution data to support research, community awareness, and policy decisions.

This work is part of the broader ‘Digital Terrascope’ initiative for smart city analytics, which also includes meteorological and traffic projects.

Key components:
- IoT Node Development
- Calibration
- Data Infrastructure
- Web Platform
- Data Analysis
